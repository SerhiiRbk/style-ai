## Post

I built Valetti because ChatGPT and “AI stylist” tools guess a season from a selfie and then invent clothes.

The colour engine measures skin, hair and iris from the pixels, then applies named rules (no mauve on a blazer; empty slot rather than a similar colour). The model does not get the last word when the measurement disagrees.

**Free:** one selfie → season and palette in about 20 seconds. No account.  
https://www.valetti.fit/colours

**Paid:** a report that puts those colours on *your* photo and says what to buy. New accounts get 6 credits (Starter Report + one try-on). Lookbook is not free.

Photos: we do not train on your face. Delete any photo in one click. GDPR Art. 9 consent is a separate tick.  
https://www.valetti.fit/trust

I am the stylist in the loop. If a look lies (a jacket you cannot buy, the wrong season), that is a bug, not a vibe.

## FAQ (have these ready)

**GDPR / photos?**  
Portrait and full-length are special-category data. We process them only after explicit consent. We do not use photos to train public models. Delete from Account → Photos, or delete the account.

**Why not women?**  
Menswear has tighter catalogue and etiquette rules (leather, dinner, office). One market until the engine is boringly right.

**Vs Colorwise / color analysis apps?**  
Those stop at a palette. The product is the recipe on your body plus real SKUs. Palette-only is the free wedge.

**Vs ChatGPT?**  
A general model will call a warm bathroom Cool Winter and draw a mauve sport coat. Rules + measurement overrule that.

**Why rules over the model?**  
Because a shared look that cannot be bought costs more than a slow launch. Empty slot > similar colour.

**Is the free palette the whole product?**  
No. Free = diagnosis. Paid = looks on you, shopping, try-on.

**Can I delete my face?**  
Yes. https://www.valetti.fit/account#photos (sign in) and the Trust page.

**Why English only for launch?**  
Persona is tech 32–45 in Prague / Warsaw / Berlin who already read HN in English. cs / pl report languages exist; marketing pages stay English until the funnel is visible.
